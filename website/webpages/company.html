Our Mission

We give enterprises
the edge they deserve

EdgeStr was founded on a simple belief: complex enterprise operations should not require armies of consultants, mountains of spreadsheets, and months of custom development. We built a platform that makes intelligence accessible to every team, in every organization.

From Fortune 500 corporations to fast-growing government contractors, our customers rely on EdgeStr to automate their most critical processes and make better decisions faster. We measure our success by theirs.
